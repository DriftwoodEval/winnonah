---
title: DDS Records Requests
---

When Disability Determination Services (DDS) faxes us a records request, the system matches it to the client, finds the client's psychological evaluation in Drive, and sends the completed reply once staff approve it.

## 1. A request appears on the Issues page

Any incoming fax headed "Disability Determination Services" is matched to a client and listed under **DDS Records to Send** in the Action Items section of the Issues page (and in the matching home page widget). These faxes do not appear in Fax Categorization.

For each request the system looks in the client's Drive folder for the most recent PDF titled "Psychological Evaluation (Confidential – Release Only with Parent/Client Permission)" and shows it on the card.

## 2. Review the request

- **Review and approve** opens the completed fax exactly as it will be sent to DDS. Check it, then click **Approve and send**, or Cancel to go back.
- **Deny** removes the request without sending anything.
- **Change PDF** lets you pick a different PDF from the client's Drive folder if the suggested one is wrong or missing.

You need the "DDS Records to Send" permission to see and act on this list.

## 3. The reply fax

After you approve, the next scheduled run sends a fax to DDS made up of:

1. A cover page: page 2 of DDS's request, with Driftwood Evaluation Center's payee name and address filled in and the authorized signature added.
2. The selected psychological evaluation PDF.

Once sent, the request leaves the list. If the fax fails to send, the request stays approved and is retried on the next run.

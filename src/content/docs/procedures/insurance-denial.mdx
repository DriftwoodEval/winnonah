## If we have submitted for a PA, completed a P2P, and otherwise exhausted our options:

Since this process requires specific knowledge of the complexities of insurance, if we have submitted for a PA, completed a P2P, and/or otherwise exhausted our options:
 
1)      Insurance specialist will review the PA Response from the insurance company. If we receive a *full denial,* insurance specialist will upload the documentation to the client’s Google Drive and flag the client using the [Insurance Review process](/docs/procedures/insurance-review) (writing a note, and changing "whose turn" to Andrew).

2)      Andrew will review the documentation to ensure that there isn’t anything else we can do. If we can do something, we stop this procedure. Otherwise:

3)      Insurance specialist will call the client to inform them that we were unable to get insurance approval. We can usually (on a case-by-case basis) offer the Private Pay route or recommend another evaluation center (MUSC, if appropriate, Charleston Neuropsych, etc.).

4)      Insurance specialist will document the "Info" tab of the EMR with specific details of the conversation with the family. Indicate whether PP was offered and/or what the family said in response to our offers.

5)      Follow our closing procedures to mark clients Inactive, archive documentation, etc.

This is **not** the punch list. The prioritization sheet ("the punch list") described in the [Questionnaire Process](/docs/procedures/questionnaires) and [Records Request Process](/docs/procedures/records-request) pages is a Google Sheet staff manage directly. The Scheduling page is a completely separate, app-native queue for a later step: once a client is ready, this is where staff line them up to actually get an evaluation appointment booked.

## 1. What it is

A dedicated page (gated by one page-level permission) showing a dense, drag-reorderable table of clients waiting to be scheduled, split into **Active** and **Archived** tabs. Only the Active tab is editable, Archived is read-only aside from bringing someone back off it.

## 2. Adding someone to the queue

A name search box adds a client with one click, that's the entire add step, no date, time, office, or evaluator is collected up front. Everything else gets filled in afterward, directly in the table.

The same add action is also available as a quick shortcut from the home dashboard, on the sections for clients whose questionnaires are done (DA, Eval, or both), so a client can be queued up in two clicks right from the dashboard without visiting the Scheduling page at all.

When a client is added, their office is guessed from whichever billing **Code** gets picked for them: choosing **96136** automatically sets the office to whichever real office is geographically closest to the client, choosing **90791** automatically sets it to **Virtual**. Either can be overridden by hand afterward.

## 3. Working the queue

Each row has: **Evaluator**, a free-text **Notes** field, **Date** and **Time**, read-only **ASD/ADHD**, **Insurance**, **District**, **PA Date**, and **Age** (all pulled from the client's own record), the **Code**, and **Location/Office**.

- Rows can be dragged to reorder, the order is saved for everyone.
- Nearly every column can be filtered, alongside a name search with next/previous match navigation. Filter choices are remembered per person.
- The **X** button on a row archives it off the Active tab. The un-archive action on the Archived tab puts a client back at the end of the Active queue and re-derives their office from their Code the same way adding one does.

## 4. Permissions

One permission gates the whole page. Unlike Insurance Review or Appointment Check-In, there's no separate, finer-grained permission for individual actions inside Scheduling, anyone who can open the page can add, edit, reorder, and archive freely.

## Edge cases

- **Loading the Scheduling page quietly refreshes three fields for every client on the punch list sheet:** ASD/ADHD, language, and who the PA is assigned to get copied from the sheet into the client record whenever they've changed. This isn't limited to clients actually in the Scheduling queue, it's a whole-sheet refresh that happens to be triggered by opening this page, it never writes anything back to the sheet.
- **Scheduling doesn't put a client on the punch list, and doesn't need them to be on it.** Getting someone onto the punch list in the first place is a separate action (the **Push to Punchlist** button on a client's Referral tab). Adding, editing, reordering, or archiving someone in the Scheduling queue never touches the punch list sheet either way.

## 2026-09-02

**Improved**
- Closest Office, and every other office-distance filter or sort in the app (the client directory's office filter, the scheduling grid, punch list reports), now reflects real by-car driving distance instead of a straight-line estimate. It's kept fresh automatically and updates right away when you reopen a client's Compare popup.

**Fixed**
- The "Compare" popup on a client's Closest Office line now lists every office with real distance and time instead of just the other offices' straight-line distance.

## 2026-09-01

**New**
- A client's page now shows a "Not Sending Questionnaires" and/or "Not Sending Records" alert whenever something is holding up automated sending, listing every reason at once: paused, an unsupported language, a missing records-needed status, a private-school client whose records need to be requested manually, records on hold, records that haven't been requested or received yet, or an unresolved delivery failure. The questionnaire half only shows up once staff have actually marked DA or EVAL Qs as needed on the prioritization sheet, so it stays out of the way for clients nothing has been asked of yet.
- The client directory's Blockers badges (previously "Unresolved Failures") now also flag those same reasons, so you can spot a blocked client without opening their page.

**Improved**
- The filename at the top of the fax review popup is now a link that opens the fax directly in Google Drive.
- Docs pages can now show callouts (note, tip, important, warning, caution, and more), so key warnings and side notes stand out from the surrounding text instead of getting lost in it.

**Fixed**
- Fax processing is better at getting scanned pages right way up. When the quick orientation check isn't sure (common on noisy faxes), it now reads the page at all four rotations and keeps whichever one comes out as real text, instead of acting on a bad guess and saving a page sideways in Drive. A page it still can't read confidently is left as received.
- Pausing a client, or checking "Autism" in Records, now also stops the automated records request from going out, matching how it already blocks questionnaires. Previously a paused client could still get a school records request sent automatically.
- Client notes now retry a save automatically when the server is briefly unreachable (for example during a deploy), instead of failing with a confusing "not valid JSON" error. If it still can't save, the message now explains the connection dropped and the note will save once it is back.
- QSuite settings (service credentials, database connection info, and appointments sync settings) are now properly restricted to staff with QSuite permissions on the server side, not just hidden from the Settings page for everyone else.
- Sending the BabyNet referral text now requires the same permission as filling out the Referral tab, on the server side, not just hidden from staff who can't fill out the tab.

## 2026-08-28

**Improved**
- The Referral tab stays editable after a client is pushed to the punchlist. The fields now hold the true, current values, and any field changed after the push is flagged with an "Edited after push" badge that shows the previous value and who changed it. Outreach and review controls stay locked once a client is on the punchlist.
- Insurance policies imported from TherapyAppointment are now limited to policies our own staff added. Policies entered by the client are no longer pulled in.
- When a client goes inactive and comes back, we now only start a fresh session (archiving their prior-session data) if they were inactive for 12 months or more. If they return sooner, their prior-session data stays put and instead their insurance review is turned on and assigned to Andrew, with a note recording when they were reactivated, when they were deactivated, and how long the gap was.

**Fixed**
- Editing or archiving one evaluator no longer briefly wipes every other evaluator off clients' eligible lists and the scheduling page. Evaluator changes now update just that evaluator's eligibility, and archived evaluators are dropped from eligible lists right away instead of lingering until the next overnight sync.

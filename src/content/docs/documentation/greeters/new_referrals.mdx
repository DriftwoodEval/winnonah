title: New Referrals to New Patients Process
---

> [!CAUTION] Note: This page is currently specifically for Alta to use for reference for this process

> [!IMPORTANT] For Alta:
> Barbara will be able to double check your work via the links in the daily report, and this should streamline the process at least a bit for both of y'all!

<big>**Input all necessary referral information from the referral into TA as usual, then follow the following process:**</big>

In the ***New Patients*** folder in Google Drive, create a new folder matching the clients name, first name then last name, located in the appropriate section of the alphabet (by first name, not last name!) 

Before returning to *NEW Referrals*, click on the three dots on the right side of the folder just created (not while inside the folder) and find *Share*, then *Copy Link*. Hold onto this link and paste it into your daily report email, next to the client's name in the list of finished referrals.

<big>**Then, to actually move the referral:**</big>

In the ***NEW Referrals*** google drive folder, click on the three dots on the right side for the client's referral. Then under *Organize*, click *Move*.

The folder you just created may be easily found in the *Suggested* tab but if it isn't there, you can search for it.

Select the newly created folder to move the PDF from *NEW Referrals* to the specific folder selected in *New Patients*. 

> [!TIP] Tip
> This process means you **do not** need to duplicate the referral pdf and move it into a “completed” folder. Just make sure everything is in the right spot in the *New Patients* folder <u>and</u> the client's folder is titled first name then last name, and in the appropriate folder based on first name.

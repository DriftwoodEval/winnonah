**Settings > Audit Log** records every change made through the app.

## What's recorded, per entry

- **Who**: the staff member who made the change, or, for background automation coming from the sibling `questionnaires` app, a system entry rather than a real account. If someone was impersonating another user when they made the change, that's shown too.
- **What**: the specific action taken. For a small submission that's already just the change (for example, changing an appointment's status), this shows the full data submitted. For an action that edits a larger stored record, such as a client, an evaluator, an insurance, a role, a person's permissions, or a settings page like [Appointment Sync](/docs/procedures/appointments-sync) or [QSuite Settings](/docs/documentation/qsuite-settings), this instead shows only what changed: added or removed entries for a list, and the old and new value for anything else. Fields that weren't touched aren't shown at all. This still means a changed credential on the QSuite Settings Services tab shows its old and new value here in full (not just that it changed), worth keeping in mind when deciding who gets access to this page.
- **Which client**: linked automatically when the app can tell an action relates to one, left blank for actions that aren't client-specific (settings changes, role changes, and so on).
- **Whether it succeeded**, and the error message if it didn't.

## Finding something

Filter by user (a dropdown of staff), by action (a dropdown of every action's internal name that appears in the log, grouped by its category, e.g. "internal", "appointments"; pick a specific action, or pick "All internal.*" at the top of a category to see every action in that category), or by client (type at least three letters of a name, then pick from the results; the selected client shows as a chip you can clear). Filters combine. 50 results per page with Previous/Next paging.

The bar across the top of every page holds navigation links and a set of informational and quick-action buttons. **Customize Header** lets you hide any of those from your own view, so the header only shows what you actually use. It changes what you see, nothing else: you keep every permission you had, and you can still reach a hidden page by typing its address.

## Opening it

Click your avatar in the top right corner, then **Customize Header**. The menu switches to the customizer panel; the **Back** arrow at the top returns to the account menu.

Anyone signed in can open it. Changes save on their own as you make them, there's no Save button.

## The panel

Every item you currently have access to is listed, with two checkboxes next to it: **Desktop** and **Mobile**. A checked box means the item shows on that layout.

- **Desktop** is the full top bar you see on a computer.
- **Mobile** is the menu behind the hamburger icon on a phone or narrow window.

Click a checkbox to show or hide that item on just that layout. Click the item's **name** instead to flip it on or off for both layouts at once. To bring a hidden item back, open this panel again and re-check its box (or click its name).

## What you can hide

**Navigation links**

| Group | Items |
|---|---|
| General | Home, Docs |
| Clients | Dashboard, Directory |
| Schedule | Day Ahead, Greeter Schedule, Availability, Scheduling |
| Reports | Claim Reports, Report Dashboard |
| Tools | Fax Categorization, Work Summary, Calculator, Questionnaire Logins |

**Actions**

Client Search, Recent Clients, Issues Alert, Task Queue, PII Redaction Toggle, Theme Switcher, Report an Issue.

Only items you can use appear in the list, so your panel may be shorter than someone else's. Report Dashboard may be listed under the evaluator's name (for example, "Andrew's Reports").

## How hidden links behave on desktop

On the desktop bar, the **Clients**, **Schedule**, **Reports**, and **Tools** groups are dropdown menus. If you hide every link in one of those groups, the whole dropdown disappears. If you hide all but one, the group collapses to a single plain link with no menu.

## Edge cases

- **Hiding an item is not the same as losing access.** The page is still there; you just navigate to it directly instead of from the header. Your permissions don't change.
- **Theme Switcher only has a Desktop checkbox.** The light/dark toggle isn't part of the mobile menu, so there's nothing to hide there.
- **A couple of items have their own show/hide rules on top of your setting.** Client Search never shows on the Home page, no matter what its box says (the Home page has its own big search). Recent Clients only shows after you've opened at least one client's page. Unchecking either one here hides it everywhere, but checking it back on doesn't override those built-in rules.
- **Desktop and Mobile are independent.** Hiding something on one layout leaves it on the other unless you also uncheck that box (or use the name toggle, which does both).

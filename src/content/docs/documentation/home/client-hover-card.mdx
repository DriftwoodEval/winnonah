---
title: Client Hover Card
---

Hover over a client's name to see a small card of their info without opening the client page. It works on the home page widgets, the Dashboard, Day Ahead, the Scheduling page, Work Summary, Fax Categorization, Reports, the Evaluator Dashboard, the Audit Log, Run Logs, reminder logs, the Client Directory, related clients on a client page, recent clients, and the Issues page.

The card appears after you rest the pointer on a name for a moment, and goes away when you move off it. Fields a client has no value for are left out of the card. The name and color dot show at the top of the card, both optional, and the indicators appear as small badges: red for high priority, yellow for paused, and grey for BabyNet and autism stop.

## Choosing what the card shows

Open **Settings**, then the **My Preferences** tab, and find the **Client Hover Card** section. Check the fields you want. The preview next to the list updates as you click, using sample data, so you can see the card before you hover a real client.

Fields you can show:

| Group | Fields |
|---|---|
| Basics | Name, color dot, age, date of birth, gender, language, evaluation type |
| Contact | Phone, email, address, school district |
| Insurance | Primary insurance, secondary insurance, PA expires, outstanding balance |
| Status | High priority indicator, paused indicator, BabyNet and autism stop indicators, next appointment, pending questionnaires, date added |

Changes save on their own as you make them, and they apply only to you.

To turn the card off, uncheck every field.

> [!INFO] Names, dates of birth, phone numbers, emails, and addresses on the card are hidden when the PII redaction toggle is on.

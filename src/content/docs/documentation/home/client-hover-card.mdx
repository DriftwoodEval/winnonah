---
title: Client Hover Card
---

<big>Hover over a client's name to see a small card of their info without opening the client page.</big>

This function works most places client names appear. The card appears after you rest the pointer on a name for a moment, and goes away when you move off it. 

> [!INFO] Fields a client has no value for are left out of the card entirely.

![client hover card gif](images/hover-card/client_hover_card.gif#w=500)

## Choosing what the card shows

Click your profile picture in the top right corner, then Settings.

To edit what the card shows open **Settings**, then **Preferences** under Personal, and find the **Client Hover Card** section at the top. 

> [!TIP] 
> To find **Settings**, click your profile picture in the top right corner, then click *Settings*.
>  
> ![](images/hover-card/find_setting.png#w=350) 

From this page, you can check and uncheck the fields you do or do not want shown on the hover card.

*The preview above the list updates in real time as you make changes, so you can see an example of what the card may look like with a real client. Fields you can show or hide are listed in the picture below.*

> [!ABSTRACT] <big>Other Important Notes & Reminders</big>
>  
>    ● <big> Changes save on their own as you make them, and they apply only to you.</big>
>  
>    ● <big> If you don't like the hover card and want to turn the card off, uncheck every field inside the settings. The card will no longer show if nothing is selected.</big>
>
>    ● <big> The client hover card is not available on mobile devices, since you do need the mouse to "hover"</big>
>  
>    ● <big> The screenshot above reflects the default selections. Click **Reset to default** to return to the default selection.</big>

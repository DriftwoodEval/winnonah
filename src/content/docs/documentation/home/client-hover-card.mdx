---
title: Client Hover Card
---

Hover over a client's name to see a small card of their info without opening the client page. It works most places client names appear. The card appears after you rest the pointer on a name for a moment, and goes away when you move off it. Fields a client has no value for are left out of the card entirely.

## Choosing what the card shows

Open **Settings**, then the **My Preferences** tab, and find the **Client Hover Card** section at the top. Check the fields you want. The preview next to the list updates as you click, using sample data, so you can see the card before you hover a real client.

The list is grouped by where each field appears on the card, top to bottom. Fields you can show:

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| Title | Name, color dot |
| Badges | Client ID, active/inactive, high priority, paused, BabyNet, autism stop, and already diagnosed indicators, outstanding balance, dashboard status |
| Quick facts | Age, gender, language, ASD/ADHD |
| Details | Date of birth, phone, email, address, school district, primary insurance, secondary insurance, PA expires, next appointment, last appointment, assigned evaluators, pending questionnaires, date added |

Changes save on their own as you make them, and they apply only to you.

To turn the card off, uncheck every field.

Each field shows whether it is on or off by default. Click **Reset to default** to return to the default selection.

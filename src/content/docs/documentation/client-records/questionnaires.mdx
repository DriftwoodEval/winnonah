title: Questionnaires
---

The **Questionnaires** link in the nav (under Clients) lists every questionnaire sent to every client in one table. Use it to answer questions like "which Conners 4 questionnaires are still pending?" or "who has been reminded three or more times?" without opening each client. You need the Questionnaires page permission to see it.

Each row is one questionnaire. It shows the client, the questionnaire, its status, the date it was sent, how many reminders have gone out, and the date of the last reminder. Click a client's name to open their page.

## Counts

The tiles above the table show how many questionnaires have each status. Click a tile to filter the table to that status, and click it again to remove the filter. The questionnaire names under the tiles work the same way and show how many of each questionnaire there are. The line above the table shows how many questionnaires match your filters and how many clients they belong to.

## Filtering and sorting

- Search by client name or questionnaire name with the search box.
- Click the filter icon on the **Client**, **Questionnaire**, **Status**, or **Reminders** column to pick one or more values. Each option shows how many rows it would match given your other filters.
- Click a column heading to sort by it. Click it again to reverse the order.
- **Clear filters** resets everything.

Archived questionnaires are hidden until you choose **Archived** in the Status filter. Filters are not saved, so the page starts fresh each time you open it.

To change a questionnaire, open the client's page. This page is for viewing only.

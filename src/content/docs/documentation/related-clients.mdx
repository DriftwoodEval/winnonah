A client's **Related Clients** card links their record to other client records, most often family sharing a household, though the system doesn't actually track what the relationship is, it's a plain connection, not a labeled one (no "sibling" or "guardian" distinction anywhere).

## Linking

The **+** button opens **Link Related Client**, a client search pre-filled with the current client's last name. Picking someone links both records to each other in one step, there's no separate "link the other direction" action needed.

The card also proactively suggests links: any other client sharing the exact same phone number on file shows up under **Suggestions** with a one-click button to link them, useful for catching family members who share a household line without anyone having to search for them.

Removing a link (hovering a linked row reveals an **X**) unlinks both directions at once.

## Where it shows up

Just the Related Clients card itself, each linked client is a clickable row over to their own record. It isn't connected to Fax Categorization's candidate-client matching, even though that feature's client-linking box looks similar, that similarity is a shared search component, not a shared relationship, fax-to-client matches and client-to-client relationships are two separate ideas that happen to reuse the same search UI.

## Edge cases

- **A client can't be linked to themselves**, that action is rejected outright.
- **The relationship carries no type or label.** If it matters why two clients are linked, that has to be tracked in notes, not in this feature.
